# Eco-Power Fit Station — bilingual demo website

A responsive Thai/English concept storefront for the Eco-Power Fit Station exercise-equipment project.

## Included
- Responsive product landing page with logo, product images, concept details, and gallery
- Thai/English language toggle
- Demo cart with quantity controls and subtotal
- Fit & Green Academy: exercise benefits, exercise types, safety, movement-to-energy concept, and habit-building tips
- Fitness timer, a short health quiz, daily movement challenge, and theoretical energy estimator
- Links to WHO physical activity guidance and Thailand's Department of Health

## Run locally
Open `index.html` in a modern browser. An internet connection is only needed for the optional Google Fonts import; the main page and SVG logo are local.

## Important before public launch
- This is a concept/demo site. The displayed price is illustrative, not a confirmed retail price.
- Cart and checkout are browser-only demonstrations. No real order is submitted and no payment is collected.
- The email form does not transmit or store submissions. Connect a secure backend before collecting customer data.
- Confirm product specifications, actual generator output, safety certification, warranty, shipping, taxes, and final pricing before advertising them as facts.
- The energy calculator is a theoretical estimate based on constant power and time, not measured performance of the product.
- Health content is general education, not individualized medical advice. The WHO guideline shown is for adults; users should adapt activity to their circumstances.
